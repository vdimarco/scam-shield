How to spot an impersonation scam

Scammers may pretend to be a bank employee, ask for a one-time code, or create a false emergency. Some demand gift cards or a wire transfer.

Never share a sign-in code with someone who calls you. If a message worries you, close it and contact your bank using the number on your card.
